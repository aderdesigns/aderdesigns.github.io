Illustrating the memories of others. This piece explores the role of the designer as a storyteller for the collective human experience. The true challenge of this competition piece was the sheer density of the brief. The task required translating a highly specific, stage-play-like text description into a single static frame. Every element — from the clustered soldier figures and the bear-head rug to the specific entry points of three distinct characters — had to be present and accurate. To solve this visual puzzle, I utilised an extreme high-angle perspective, allowing me to map out the complex spatial relationships and fit every required constraint into the composition without losing the ominous, sunlit atmosphere.
