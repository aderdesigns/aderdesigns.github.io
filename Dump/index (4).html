OH HEY,
I'M ADARSH (ader)

Designer by craft. Story Hunter. Frugal Traveller.
I make things that mean something — because I think
the story behind a creation is the creation.

I'm a Communication Designer from IIT Bombay with a background in Architecture — which means I've always thought about how spaces, systems, and stories hold people.

I work across branding, packaging, editorial, and data visualization, but the thread running through all of it is the same: find the real story, then build something around it that couldn't exist any other way.

I travel frugally on purpose. Slow trains, budget stays, unfamiliar places — that's where empathy gets built. It shows up in my work more than any software ever could.

I don't do generic. I collaborate deeply, research obsessively, and design for the story first.
